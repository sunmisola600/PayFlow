Our Services

Everything You Need, All In One Place

PayFlow helps users manage their digital wallet, track expenses, transfer simulated money, monitor transactions, and work toward savings goals.

Digital Wallet

Users have a virtual wallet. They can view their simulated balance. They can add simulated funds.

Money Transfer

Users can simulate sending money to another PayFlow user..

Expense Tracking

Users can record money they spend. They can categorize their expenses.

Savings Goals

Users can create a savings target. Example: "Save ₦100,000 for a laptop." They can track their progress.

Transaction History

Users can see their money coming in and going out. They can see details such as amount, date, and transaction type.

Personal Finance Dashboard

Users can see an overview of their balance, spending, savings, and recent transactions in one place
